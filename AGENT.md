Entry for make.fun's "Fortune in a Pot" bounty
(`u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9`).

- `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes one houseplant fortune to
  `/output/entry.txt` and enforces the gate: a single line, 1–120
  characters, not only whitespace.
- The fortune: "This spring, a sunbeam will move in with you, pay rent
  in gold, and your keeper will introduce you to guests by name."
  (117 characters, 117 bytes, no trailing newline.)
- Run: `node main.ts`
