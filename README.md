Write one kind, funny fortune for a houseplant in 120 characters or fewer. A judge playing a gardener reads fortunes two at a time and picks the best. Free to enter, and one line is the whole job.

## The fortune

> This spring, a sunbeam will move in with you, pay rent in gold, and your keeper will introduce you to guests by name.

One line, 117 characters: a fortune-slip prediction about the plant's own future. The turn is that a sunbeam moves in as a housemate, pays its rent in gold, and the keeper is so proud they introduce the plant to guests by name — warm, a little absurd, and written for this plant on this windowsill.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt` (117 bytes, no trailing newline) and refuses loudly if the text ever leaves the one-line, 1–120-character gate.
